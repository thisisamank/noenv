Roles

RoleCan do
readerLoad secrets into the shell
editorLoad and write secrets, and roll back versions
adminAll of the above, and manage members and policies

1. Invite a person

Invite from Team in the dashboard, from the Members card of a project, or from the CLI:

noenv invite sam@acme.com

You get a link to send. If invitation emails are on, noenv emails the link for you.

2. The person joins

  1. They open the link and create a passkey.
  2. They install the CLI: npm install -g @thisisamank/noenv-cli.
  3. They connect their shell and approve the terminal.

3. Share the environment key

Run noenv grant in the repository, after the person has connected their shell:

~/code/api ❯ noenv grant sam@acme.com editor

This gives the role on the project and shares the key for the current environment. Each environment has its own key. To share production, select it first:

NOENV_ENVIRONMENT=production noenv grant sam@acme.com reader
Key sharing needs your private key, which lives only in ~/.config/noenv on your machine. That is why noenv grant runs in the CLI and not in the dashboard. The dashboard shows who holds a key for each environment.

Cancel an invite

An invite that nobody has used yet can be cancelled. Click Cancel invite next to the person in Overview or Team. The invite link stops working at once, and the person is removed from every project they were added to.

You can only cancel an invite for a person who has not joined. For a person who already has a passkey, remove them from the project instead.

Remove a person

Remove them from the project Members card, or run:

noenv revoke sam@acme.com
Removing access stops future loads. It cannot take back values that the person already loaded in their shell. After you remove someone, change the important values with noenv secret set.

Invitation emails

noenv can email invites through Cloudflare Email Sending. Without it, you send the link yourself.

  1. Onboard your domain: npx wrangler email sending enable example.com.
  2. Set the sender address: npx wrangler secret put EMAIL_FROM, for example noenv@example.com.
